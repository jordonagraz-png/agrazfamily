Our story

Home is wherever we’re together.

We’re the Agraz family — parents and children, grandparents and grandkids, cousins, in-laws, and the friends who became family along the way.

Life takes each of us in different directions, but we always find our way back to each other — to the long dinners, the loud laughter, and the stories that get better every time they’re told.

This little corner of the internet is our front porch: a place to welcome friends, share a bit of who we are, and keep the light on for one another.

Our name

A name that’s still growing.

In Spanish, agraz is the young grape — the fruit that hasn’t finished becoming what it’s going to be.

We think that’s a fitting word for a family. Every generation adds something new: a recipe, a tradition, a story, a face at the table. We carry the roots of the ones who came before us, and we’re never quite done growing.

What we hold dear

Four things we always come back to.

  1. 01

    Gather often

    The table is always big enough. Birthdays, holidays, or a regular Tuesday — any excuse to be together is a good one.

  2. 02

    Remember well

    We tell the old stories so the youngest know where they come from — and so the ones we’ve lost are never far away.

  3. 03

    Show up

    For the big days and the hard ones. That’s the deal, and nobody ever has to ask twice.

  4. 04

    Keep growing

    New chapters, new faces, new traditions. Different branches, the same roots.
